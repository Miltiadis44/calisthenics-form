My Story

I have been working out my whole life, gym, bodyweight, you name it. But in 2022 everything changed. I committed fully to calisthenics and never looked back.

Even though my body was getting stronger, the lack of proper guidance was holding me back. I was getting injured often and my form was far from optimal, because your body learns exactly how you teach it, and without the right technique and consistency, bad habits build up fast. That changed in June 2025 when I started training under Christian Mariani, one of the best calisthenics athletes in the world. He showed me the correct and most effective path, the road to perfection.

In 2026 I became an International Champion in Strength and Endurance Calisthenics. That journey taught me the value of clean execution, smart programming and proper adaptation. Now I want to share that with you, a program built around the calisthenics mindset, correct movement patterns, and progressive overload that makes you genuinely stronger. The physique is just what follows.
